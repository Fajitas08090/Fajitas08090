I'm Maxime Renac, a data manager based in Barcelona, working at the intersection of **data engineering**, **analytics**, **machine learning**, and **e-commerce**. I own the full data stack of a multi-brand wine retail group (Le Petit Ballon, Decantalo, Bodeboca), from ingestion to dashboards, data products and ML models.

My work usually revolves around turning messy, heterogeneous business data into reliable pipelines, clear metrics, and useful data products. As a data professional, I've learned to keep things **simple, observable, and maintainable**.

I use this space to share **data projects**, **internal tooling**, **self-hosting experiments**, and other things I build.

#### What I work on

- data warehousing and transformation (BigQuery, dbt, Airflow, Python)
- subscription and e-commerce analytics (churn, cohorts, conversion, market basket analysis, AB Test)
- demand forecasting and intermittent demand modelling (LightGBM, Prophet, MLflow)
- pricing analytics (price history, competitor monitoring, elasticity with GLM Poisson)
- marketing analytics (GA4 to BigQuery, campaign performance, email impact analysis)
- self analytics tools (datawarehouse to Claude, designing skills and connectors)
- data governance, data catalogs, and data culture training for non-technical teams
- LLM-based tooling and RAG systems that teams can adapt to their own needs
- Streamlit apps, Looker Studio dashboards, and lightweight internal platforms

Over the years I've built a sales forecasting system covering 300+ SKUs, a churn prediction model for subscribers, a price elasticity monitoring platform, and led the migration of a group's data infrastructure from Magento and Shopify to Odoo, with pipelines across heterogeneous schemas.

I also enjoy the practical engineering side of things: **home lab setups**, **Docker**, **Home Assistant**, **DNS-level ad blocking**, and self-hosted apps running on a home NAS.
